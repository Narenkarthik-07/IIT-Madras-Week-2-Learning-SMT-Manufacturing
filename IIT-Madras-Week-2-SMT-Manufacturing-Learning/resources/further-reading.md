# Further Reading

The following are useful starting points for learning more about SMT assembly. Check that the selected document matches the relevant equipment, solder paste, and process before applying its guidance.

1. **Solder paste supplier technical data** — product datasheets, storage and handling instructions, stencil-printing guidance, and recommended reflow profiles. Begin with the supplier of the paste used in a specific process.
2. **Stencil and printer documentation** — machine-specific setup, alignment, cleaning, maintenance, and safety guidance from the equipment manufacturer.
3. **SPI manufacturer resources** — application notes explaining 2D/3D solder paste inspection, measurement definitions, recipe setup, and defect analysis.
4. **Pick-and-place manufacturer resources** — documentation on component data, feeders, nozzles, fiducials, programming, and machine safety.
5. **Reflow equipment and solder-paste guidance** — profile development, thermal measurement, and process-window considerations.
6. **AOI manufacturer resources** — inspection programming, lighting, false-call management, and limitations of optical inspection.
7. **IPC standards information** — consult the current applicable standards and licensing terms for assembly acceptability, design, and process requirements.

These categories are deliberately provided rather than unverified deep links or claims about a particular machine configuration. A specific reference should be added only after its title, publisher, revision, and URL have been checked.
